Scarcity Decision Tree

What's the goal?

├── Utilization (don't waste capacity)
│   └── Shape of the problem?
│       ├── Uneven task duration       → Work Stealing
│       ├── Many small operations      → Batching
│       └── Variable load over time    → Adaptive Sizing

└── Governance (don't exceed limits)
    └── What are you protecting?
        ├── Concurrent operations
        │   └── Handing out actual objects? (connections, GPU)?
        │       ├── Yes → BlockingQueue as resource pool
        │       └── No  → Semaphore with N permits
        └── Aggregate consumption      → Semaphore where permits = resource units
